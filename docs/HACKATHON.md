## Participation and submission

The [guidelines](https://cyberhackathon.bihar.gov.in/guidelines) require 3–4 enrolled students, individual college IDs, and one team/problem per participant/team. The leader creates a private GitHub repository named after the team, with all members as collaborators. Iterative commits are mandatory; a final code dump is disqualifying. The stack is unrestricted, subject to challenge constraints.

The [FAQ](https://cyberhackathon.bihar.gov.in/faq) requires an 8–10 minute explanation/demo video, maximum 10 minutes, covering the problem, architecture, implementation, and working solution. Upload it to the team's Google Drive, grant Viewer access to `hackathonccsubihar@gmail.com`, and submit its URL through the portal. Clear voice-over suffices; faces are optional.
